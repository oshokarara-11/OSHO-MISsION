🌱 Patient Recovery & Daily Routine Guide

1. Poori Neend aur Subah Jaldi Uthna: Raat ko time se soyein taaki neend poori ho. Subah early morning fresh mind ke sath uthna recovery ke liye sabse pehla aur zaroori kadam hai.

2. Meditation aur Prayer: Subah uthkar meditation aur prayer karein. Isse patient mentally bahut motivated rehta hai aur andar se positive energy milti hai.

3. Yog (Anulom Vilom aur Brahmri): Yog mein Anulom Vilom aur Brahmri pranayam karein, jo patient ki mental aur physical health ke liye ekdum ramban hai.

4. Halki Exercise aur Sahi Khana: Thodi bahut light exercise karein aur healthy, poshtik ghar ka khana khayein jo body ko jaldi recover kare.

5. Future aur Study Goals: Agar patient student hai, toh apni health ko dhyan mein rakhte hue dheere-dheere apni study aur future goals par focus kare.
